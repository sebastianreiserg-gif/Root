Speech audio
This app plays pre-generated audio clips rather than synthesizing speech live. Export the checklist below, generate each clip with whatever tool you're using per language (see your notes), then drop the files into the matching folder — playback picks them up automatically, no app update needed.
Download audio checklist
Every sentence × dialect, with exact filenames
Two file types: sentence clips (audio/{lang}/{dialect}/{unit-id}-{n}.mp3) and individual word/phrase clips, deduplicated across the whole language (audio/{lang}/{dialect}/words/{word-slug}.mp3). The checklist CSV has a "type" column and the exact real spelling for each — filenames strip accents for safety, but always feed the real spelling into your TTS tool.
